Send Athena to a meeting

Paste a Zoom link, or the whole invite. Athena joins on the server as a guest called “Athena”, records each person under their Zoom name and leaves when the meeting ends. Nothing on this computer records, and this tab can be closed.

Join the meeting yourself too — Athena records while you're in it.

Or record it on this computer

Records a meeting's audio on this computer and sends it to Athena, which transcribes and summarizes it like any other conversation. Keep this tab open while you record.

  • Meeting in a browser tab (Zoom web, Google Meet, Teams web): choose that tab and turn on Also share tab audio.
  • Zoom or another app: choose its window or the entire screen and turn on Also share system audio (Windows; macOS 14.2 or newer with Chrome 141 or newer; Linux depends on the desktop, try it once).
  • The video is not recorded or sent, only the sound.
Use headphones, or the microphone also picks up the other people.
